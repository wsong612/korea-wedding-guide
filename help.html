FAQ

Questions guests may ask

Should I stay in Seoul or near the wedding venue?

For most guests, Seoul is the best base. Stay near the venue or in Cheongju if you prefer a calmer wedding day or are helping with preparations.

What time is the wedding?

The wedding is on 3 April 2027. The exact time will be added once confirmed.

When should I RSVP?

Please RSVP by the end of January 2027. The current planning deadline is 31 January 2027.

Can I use Google Maps in Korea?

Google Maps can be useful as a backup, but Naver Map and KakaoMap are usually better for local navigation.

Do I need cash?

Cards are widely useful, but bring or withdraw some Korean won for markets, small shops, emergencies, and some transport-card situations.

Is Korea vegetarian-friendly?

It is possible, but less straightforward than in many European cities. Broths, sauces, and side dishes may contain seafood or meat ingredients.

Who should I contact if I have trouble getting to the venue?

Contact the wedding contact listed above. Add a real contact name, phone number, WhatsApp link, and backup contact before publishing.
