FAQ

Common guest questions

Should I stay in Seoul or near the wedding venue?

For sightseeing, Seoul is the best base. If you want an easier wedding morning, stay in Cheongju the night before. If you join the barbecue after-party, consider Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest for one night after the wedding.

What should I wear?

Elegant wedding guest attire with comfortable shoes and a spring layer. Hanbok is welcome but not required.

Can I rent Hanbok?

Yes, Hanbok rental at the venue is expected to be available for around ₩3,000. We will confirm exact details later.

Do I need to bring a gift?

Korean weddings commonly use cash envelopes. For European guests, €50–€100 per attending adult is a reasonable simple guideline, with close family or very close friends sometimes giving more.

Can I use Google Maps?

Google Maps can help, but Naver Map and KakaoMap are usually better for Korean addresses and local routing.

Can I get by with English?

In Seoul and airports, usually yes. In countryside areas, translation apps and Korean addresses are very helpful.

What if I join the Jeju trip?

Please indicate this in the main RSVP form. We will plan flights and accommodation once we know the group size.
