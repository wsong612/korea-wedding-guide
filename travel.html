Korea basics

Practical information

Refresh this section shortly before guests travel, especially visa/K-ETA, transport cards, and weather.

₩

Money

Currency: Korean won / KRW. Cards are widely useful, but small cash can still help at markets, for some top-ups, and emergencies.

🔌

Electricity

Korea uses 220V / 60Hz and round two-hole outlets. Most European devices work, but check your charger and adapter needs.

🌦️

Weather

Early April is spring. Bring layers, a light jacket, comfortable shoes, and an umbrella. Check the forecast close to departure.

🚫

Tipping

Tipping is generally not expected in Korea. Restaurants and taxis usually do not require tips.

Transport

Getting around Korea

Subway & buses

Use a transport card where possible. Follow station exit numbers carefully; they matter in large stations.

Taxis

Kakao T is useful, but guests should also save Korean addresses in case they need to show them directly to a driver.

Trains & intercity buses

Useful for longer trips beyond Seoul. Add booking links and route examples later.

Packing

What to bring

Wedding

Wedding outfit, warm layer, comfortable shoes, small bag, and any printed address/contact card.

Travel

Passport, travel insurance, card, some cash, adapter, medication, and offline copies of key bookings.

Spring weather

Light jacket, layered clothing, umbrella, sunglasses, and allergy medicine if you are sensitive to pollen.
