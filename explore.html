Seoul

Things to do in Seoul

Use these as starting points when choosing accommodation and planning free days before or after the wedding.

Historic Seoul

Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Insadong, and Ikseon-dong.

Modern Seoul

Seongsu, Gangnam, The Hyundai Seoul, Lotte World Tower, COEX, and Starfield Library.

Markets & food

Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market, Mangwon Market, Myeongdong street food, and café hopping.

Nature & views

Namsan Seoul Tower, Han River parks, Seoul Forest, and Bukhansan for active guests.

Nightlife

Hongdae, Itaewon, Yeonnam, Seongsu, Apgujeong, and Sinsa.

Traditional experience

Hanbok rental, tea houses, palace visits, and craft workshops.

Beyond Seoul

Other places to consider

These are optional ideas for guests who want to extend the trip beyond Seoul and the wedding area.

Busan

Sea, markets, cafés, seafood, and a relaxed city atmosphere.

Gyeongju

History, temples, royal tombs, and a strong traditional atmosphere.

Jeju

Nature, coast, volcanic landscapes, and the planned group trip after the wedding.

See Jeju group trip page

Jeonju

Hanok village, food, and a slower traditional city feel.

Sokcho / Seoraksan

Mountains, nature, and hiking.

Suwon / DMZ

Suwon is an easy cultural day trip; DMZ tours need advance booking and ID.

Itineraries

Example trip lengths

3 days

Arrival, one day in Seoul, wedding day.

5 days

Seoul, wedding, and one easy day trip or extra Seoul day.

7–10 days

Seoul, wedding, Jeju group trip or Gyeongju/Busan.

2 weeks

Seoul, wedding, Jeju, Gyeongju, Busan, and possibly Seoraksan or Jeonju.
