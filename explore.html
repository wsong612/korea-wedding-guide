Explore Korea

Enjoy the trip

Food, culture, Seoul neighborhoods, day trips, and longer itinerary ideas for guests who want to turn the wedding into a Korea holiday.

Culture

Korean manners & etiquette

A friendly guide, not a strict rulebook. Small gestures go a long way.

Greetings

A light bow or polite nod is common. Giving and receiving things with two hands feels respectful.

Public transport

Keep voices low, queue calmly, and avoid eating on subways or buses.

Shoes & traditional spaces

You may need to remove shoes in homes, some restaurants, hanok stays, or traditional spaces.

Restaurants

Sharing dishes is common. Some restaurants specialize in one type of food, and staff may not speak much English.

Wedding gifts

Add your final guidance on envelope, card, or no gift once decided.

Trash

Public bins can be harder to find than in Europe. Keep small trash with you until you find a bin.

Food

Food & dietary guide

Foods to try

Korean BBQ, bibimbap, kimbap, jeon, kalguksu, Korean fried chicken, tteokbokki, temple food, market snacks, and café desserts.

Dietary restrictions

Vegetarian and allergy needs can be more complicated in Korea because broth, fish sauce, seafood, or meat garnish may be hidden. Add Korean phrase cards here.

Please tell us early: allergies, vegetarian/vegan diet, pregnancy-related restrictions, gluten issues, nut allergies, seafood allergies, or alcohol restrictions by 31 January 2027.

Seoul guide

Things to do in Seoul

Organized by travel style so guests can quickly choose what fits them.

Historic Seoul

Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Bukchon Hanok Village, Insadong, Ikseon-dong, Jogyesa Temple.

Modern Seoul

Gangnam, Seongsu, The Hyundai Seoul, Lotte World Tower, COEX / Starfield Library.

Markets & food

Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market, Mangwon Market, Myeongdong street food.

Nature & views

Namsan Seoul Tower, Han River parks, Seoul Forest, Bukhansan for active guests.

Nightlife & cafés

Hongdae, Itaewon, Yeonnam, Seongsu, Apgujeong, Sinsa.

Beyond Seoul

Trips before or after the wedding

Busan

Seaside city, markets, seafood, cafés, beaches, and a relaxed atmosphere.

Gyeongju

History, temples, royal tombs, and one of the best cultural trips for first-time visitors.

Jeju

Nature, volcanic landscapes, beaches, waterfalls, and a slower driving trip.

Jeonju

Hanok village, food, traditional atmosphere, and a slower city break.

Sokcho / Seoraksan

Mountains, hiking, nature, and seafood.

Suwon / DMZ

Good day-trip options from Seoul, depending on interests and booking availability.
