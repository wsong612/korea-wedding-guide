Korea basics

Practical information

Final version should be refreshed shortly before guests travel, especially visa/K-ETA, transport cards, and weather.

₩

Money

Currency: Korean won / KRW. Cards are widely useful, but small cash can still help at markets, for some top-ups, and emergencies.

🔌

Electricity

Korea uses 220V / 60Hz and round two-hole outlets. Most European devices work, but check your charger and adapter needs.

🌦️

Weather

Early April is spring. Bring layers, a light jacket, comfortable shoes, and an umbrella. Check the forecast close to departure.

🚫

Tipping

Tipping is generally not expected in Korea. Restaurants and taxis usually do not require tips.

Culture

Korean manners & etiquette

A friendly guide, not a strict rulebook. Small gestures go a long way.

Greetings

A light bow or polite nod is common. Giving and receiving things with two hands feels respectful.

Public transport

Keep voices low, queue calmly, and avoid eating on subways or buses.

Shoes & traditional spaces

You may need to remove shoes in homes, some restaurants, hanok stays, or traditional spaces.

Restaurants

Sharing dishes is common. Some restaurants specialize in one type of food, and staff may not speak much English.

Wedding gifts

We will explain whether guests should bring an envelope, a card, or nothing at all.

Trash

Public bins can be harder to find than in Europe. Keep small trash with you until you find a bin.

FAQ

Questions guests may ask

Should I stay in Seoul or near the wedding venue?

For most guests, Seoul is the best base. Stay near the venue or in Cheongju if you prefer a calmer wedding day or are helping with preparations.

Can I use Google Maps in Korea?

Google Maps can be useful as a backup, but Naver Map and KakaoMap are usually better for local navigation.

Do I need cash?

Cards are widely useful, but bring or withdraw some Korean won for markets, small shops, emergencies, and some transport-card situations.

Is Korea vegetarian-friendly?

It is possible, but less straightforward than in many European cities. Broths, sauces, and side dishes may contain seafood or meat ingredients.

What if I miss the shuttle?

Contact the wedding coordinator immediately. We will add backup taxi/private transfer instructions here.
