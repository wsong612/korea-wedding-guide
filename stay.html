Where to stay

Choose your base before and after the wedding

Most guests can use Seoul as their travel base, but the wedding itself is in the Cheongju area. If you join the barbecue after-party, staying near the groom’s parents’ place may be easier.

Seoul, by mood

Neighborhood guide for accommodation

Photos will make this page much easier for guests choosing where to sleep in a country they may not know yet.

Myeongdong / Euljiro

Practical and tourist-friendly. Good for first-time visitors who want easy sightseeing and food options.

Jongno / Insadong

Good for palaces, traditional streets, tea houses, and a calmer cultural base.

Hongdae / Yeonnam

Fun, lively, and good for younger friends, cafés, casual restaurants, and nightlife.

Itaewon / Hannam

International food, bars, and an easier environment for mixed international groups.

Gangnam / Sinnonhyeon

Modern Seoul, shopping, nightlife, and easy taxis, though farther from older Seoul.

Seoul Station / Gongdeok

Very practical for airport and train connections. Less atmospheric, but convenient.

After the wedding

Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest

For guests joining the barbecue after-party, this is the recommended one-night stay because it is right next to the groom’s parents’ place.

Why here?

The after-party may continue until late, so staying nearby is more comfortable than returning to Seoul the same night.
