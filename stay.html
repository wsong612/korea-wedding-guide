Decision guide

Which base makes sense?

Stay in Seoul

Best if you are sightseeing before/after the wedding and are comfortable arranging intercity travel to the Cheongju area on the wedding day.

Stay in Cheongju before the wedding

Best if you prefer a calmer wedding morning and want to reduce same-day travel from Seoul.

Stay near the after-party

Best if you plan to join the barbecue after-party at the groom’s parents’ place after the ceremony.

Before the wedding

If not staying in Seoul

Option 1: Hotels in Cheongju

Cheongju is the practical city base near the venue. This is likely easier for guests who want normal hotel comfort, private bathrooms, luggage convenience, and taxi access to the ceremony.

Specific hotel recommendations and booking links will be added later.

Option 2: Hanok-style stay at Chojeong Haenggung

The venue itself has hanok-style accommodation. This could be a memorable option for guests who want a traditional experience close to the ceremony.

Availability is limited, so this should be coordinated rather than booked casually.

After the wedding

Recommended: Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest for one night

After the ceremony, there will be a barbecue after-party at the groom’s parents’ place. Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest (생거진천자연휴양림) is right next to the parents’ place, so it is the recommended option for guests joining the after-party.

Recommended after-party stay

Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest

A natural recreation forest accommodation area in Jincheon. It should be treated as a practical one-night stay near the after-party rather than a Seoul-style hotel.

Seoul bases

Where to stay in Seoul before or after Korea travel

Myeongdong / Euljiro

Good for first-time visitors, shopping, central sightseeing, and easy food options.

Jongno / Insadong

Good for palaces, traditional streets, older relatives, and a more cultural atmosphere.

Hongdae / Yeonnam

Good for younger guests, cafés, nightlife, and a more casual city feel.

Itaewon / Hannam

Good for international restaurants and bars.

Gangnam / Sinnonhyeon

Good for modern Seoul, shopping, and nightlife, though farther from older palace areas.

Seoul Station / Gongdeok

Practical for airport rail access and transport connections.
