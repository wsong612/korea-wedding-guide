Accommodation

Where to stay

A practical guide to choosing between Seoul, Cheongju, and accommodation closer to the venue.

Simple recommendation

Stay in Seoul unless you prefer a slower wedding day

Seoul is usually the easiest base for sightseeing, food, shopping, and international arrivals. Cheongju or nearby stays can be better for family members, older guests, or anyone who wants to avoid a long same-day journey.

Best for most guests: Seoul

Choose Seoul if you want convenient sightseeing, restaurants, cafés, nightlife, and easier arrival/departure logistics.

Choose Cheongju / nearby if:

You prefer a calmer wedding day, are travelling with older relatives, dislike early travel, or are part of the wedding preparation group.

Seoul neighborhoods

Recommended areas

Myeongdong / Euljiro

Best for first-time visitors, shopping, central sightseeing, and easy taxi/subway access.

Jongno / Insadong

Best for palaces, traditional Seoul, older relatives, tea houses, and cultural walks.

Hongdae / Yeonnam

Best for younger friends, cafés, casual nightlife, music, and a lively atmosphere.

Itaewon / Hannam

Best for international food, bars, and guests who want familiar food options nearby.

Gangnam / Sinnonhyeon

Best for modern Seoul, shopping, beauty clinics, nightlife, and a polished city feel.

Seoul Station / Gongdeok

Best for airport access and practical transport connections.
