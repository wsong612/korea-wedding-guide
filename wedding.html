Venue

Chojeong Haenggung / 초정행궁

Chojeong Haenggung is a hanok-style venue and cultural site in the Cheongju area. It is outside central Seoul, so guests should plan the wedding-day route carefully.

View VisitKorea venue page

Ceremony style

What to expect at a traditional Korean wedding

This will be different from a typical European church or town-hall wedding. The ceremony is likely to feel more ceremonial, visual, and rooted in traditional Korean symbolism.

Traditional clothing

The bride and groom will wear traditional Korean wedding clothing. Guests do not need to wear Hanbok, but Hanbok is welcome if they would like to participate in the atmosphere.

Symbolic ceremony

You may see formal bows, ceremonial tables, symbolic foods or objects, and ritual movements. The meaning is celebration, respect, and the joining of families.

Hanbok rental

Hanbok can be rented at the venue for approximately ₩3,000. We will confirm availability, sizes, and timing closer to the wedding.

Dress code

Elegant, respectful, and comfortable for spring

Recommended

Smart wedding guest attire, spring layers, comfortable shoes, and a light jacket. The venue has a traditional countryside feel, so avoid very fragile shoes or anything difficult to walk in.

Hanbok welcome

Guests may wear Hanbok if they wish. It is not required. If renting Hanbok at the venue, plan extra time before the ceremony.

Avoid

Very casual clothes, sportswear, beachwear, or anything that would feel disrespectful in wedding photos. A fully white outfit is best avoided as a guest.

Weather note

Early April can be mild but still cool in the morning or evening. Bring a layer, especially if staying for the barbecue after-party.

Directions

Getting to Chojeong Haenggung

We will provide final independent travel guidance closer to the date.

From Seoul

Plan for intercity travel to the Cheongju area, then a local taxi or pickup arrangement to the venue. Final step-by-step instructions will be added later.

From Cheongju

Cheongju hotels are the practical non-Seoul option before the wedding. From there, the venue will likely be easiest by taxi.

After-party

After-party with barbecue at the groom’s parents’ place

After the traditional wedding, there will be an informal barbecue after-party at the groom’s parents’ place. It may continue until late at night, so staying nearby can be more comfortable than returning to Seoul.

Why stay nearby?

Because the barbecue may finish late, guests who join the after-party may prefer to stay nearby rather than return to Seoul the same night.

Recommended nearby stay

Saenggeo Jincheon Natural Recreation Forest is right next to the parents’ place and is the recommended after-wedding accommodation option for one night.
